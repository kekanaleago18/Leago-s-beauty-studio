Membership

Become a Leago Beauty Studio member and enjoy exclusive benefits, discounted rates, and priority booking. Choose the membership tier that suits you best.

Membership Tiers

Silver Membership

Price: R450 per month

  • One facial or manicure per month
  • 10% discount on additional treatments
  • Priority booking

Gold Membership

Price: R750 per month

  • One facial and one massage per month
  • 15% discount on additional treatments

Platinum Membership

Price: R1200 per month

Why Become a Member?

Membership at Leago Beauty Studio is designed for clients who want to make self-care a regular part of their routine. Enjoy consistent, high-quality treatments at discounted rates, along with priority access to booking slots.
